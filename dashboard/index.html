How the model works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × chain breadth. The category multiple is the shrunk median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live protocols in that category; momentum saturates with the 7-day change; maturity grows with age; breadth adds a small factor for each additional chain.
